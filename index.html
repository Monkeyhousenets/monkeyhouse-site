What's a Treenet?

A handwoven world between your trees

Picture a giant net woven from climbing-grade rope, strung between the trees in your backyard. You can bounce on it, climb all over it, nap in it — and it's all built by hand, on-site, shaped around whatever your trees make possible.

Every piece of hardware is rated for climbing. The whole structure is engineered to hold — as solid as the oaks it hangs from.

"It's bouncy like a trampoline, loungy like a hammock. It fits in the environment and it just feels like you're at home in a tree."

As Strong as Your Trees

The rated rope and hardware climbers trust with their lives.

Tree-Safe

Wooden spacers keep the rope off the bark, so your trees stay healthy.

Handwoven

Every knot tied on-site by hand.
